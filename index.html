Our standards

Fast doesn't mean careless.

ScoopEarth moves quickly, but every story goes through the same four checks before it's published.

  1. 01

    Confirm the source

    We trace a story back to where it actually originated before covering it.
  2. 02

    Separate fact from take

    Reporting and opinion are labeled clearly, never blended.
  3. 03

    Update, don't bury

    When a story changes, we edit it in the open with a visible update note.
  4. 04

    Keep it clean

    General news and culture only — no casino, gambling, or adult content, ever.
